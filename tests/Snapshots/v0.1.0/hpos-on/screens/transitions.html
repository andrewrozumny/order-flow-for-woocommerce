Control who may move an order into or out of your custom statuses.

  • Changes between two WooCommerce statuses are never restricted.
  • A change into or out of a custom status needs a matching rule that lists one of the user's roles.
  • Administrators can always make any change.
  • Rules apply to people working in wp-admin (order screen, bulk actions, Order Flow buttons). Gateways, the REST API, cron and WP-CLI are not blocked.
  • "Require comment" asks for a note with the change. Bulk actions skip such orders.
